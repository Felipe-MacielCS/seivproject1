CYBER SECURITY

Cybersecurity is a crucial emphasis of computer science that focuses on protecting digital systems, networks, and data from unauthorized access, attacks, and damage. It combines technical expertise, risk management, and security protocols to defend against threats such as malware, hacking, and data breaches. As technology continues to advance and society becomes more reliant on digital infrastructure, cybersecurity plays an essential role in ensuring privacy, safety, and trust in the digital world.
