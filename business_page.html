BUSINESS

Business as an emphasis of computer science focuses on applying technology to improve efficiency, decision-making, and innovation within organizations. It involves areas such as data analytics, information systems, and software solutions that help companies streamline operations, understand markets, and enhance customer experiences. By combining technical skills with business knowledge, this emphasis prepares professionals to bridge the gap between technology and organizational strategy in today’s digital economy.
