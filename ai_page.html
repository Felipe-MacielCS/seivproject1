ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Artificial intelligence (AI) as an emphasis of computer science focuses on developing systems that can mimic human intelligence through learning, reasoning, and problem-solving. It covers areas such as machine learning, natural language processing, robotics, and computer vision, all aimed at creating smarter and more adaptive technologies. With applications ranging from healthcare to self-driving cars, AI represents one of the most innovative and impactful areas of computer science today.
